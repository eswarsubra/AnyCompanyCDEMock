## How to read this report

- **Mean judge score** is the pipeline's own fidelity+fluency rating (same prompt/model/threshold the deployed quality stage uses). Higher is better; translations below the keep threshold are filtered before the API serves them.
- **Mean back-translation similarity** (0–1) round-trips each translation back to its source language and lexically compares it to the original. It is an independent, deterministic sanity check: a low value flags a translation whose meaning did not survive the round trip.
- **% kept** is the share of translations at or above the keep threshold — the conversion-relevant signal for how much translated content actually reaches the product page.
